A person, not a pizza chain

Made by Mayank.
Made with care.

Mayank Patel went to Italy to learn the craft behind a proper pizza. Now he’s bringing that spirit to Baroda — with slow-fermented sourdough, carefully cooked sauces and a belief that the best things shouldn’t be rushed.

M’s Sourdough is a small, personal project — not a production line. Just good dough, a hot oven, and the kind of meal you want to sit down and enjoy.
